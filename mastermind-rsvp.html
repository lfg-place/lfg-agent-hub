Brian Gubernick is a real estate entrepreneur, endurance athlete, keynote speaker, girl dad, and host of the No Days Off podcast. Over the past 15+ years, he has worn nearly every hat in the real estate industry — agent, investor, sales team owner, coach, brokerage leader, and corporate executive — building and scaling businesses that have impacted thousands nationwide. He is currently the Chief Real Estate Officer at PLACE, an Advisor to Homeward, and a partner in multiple real estate-related ventures, including property management, title, and investment firms.

Brian began his journey in 2007 with the launch of Homehelper Consultants (HhC), a nationally recognized team within Keller Williams, earning accolades such as the 2014 Keller Williams Innovator Award and recognition from Inman in 2017. That same year, he founded Metrix, which has since mentored hundreds of real estate leaders. In Spring 2025, he delivered a powerful TEDx talk on the urgency of living intentionally, challenging others to embrace purpose, presence, and bold action.

A lifelong competitor, Brian channels his passion for growth through endurance sports — having completed marathons, ultramarathons, and 50K races — as a way to cultivate mental resilience and personal transformation.
